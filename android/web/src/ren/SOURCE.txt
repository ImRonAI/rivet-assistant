These files are never edited here (SHA256SUMS holds their hashes; `npm run check:ren` verifies them). The file list is
the import closure of src/pages/SearchResultsPage.tsx, src/pages/HomePage.tsx, src/pages/OnboardingPage.tsx, the pages
App.tsx's MainContent draws for Projects and Documents, its sign-in page and of the
components ren desktop's agent panel (src/components/agent-panel/AgentPanel.tsx) draws a conversation with:
ai-elements/conversation, message, response, chain-of-thought, tool and shimmer:

  esbuild src/pages/SearchResultsPage.tsx --bundle --packages=external --metafile=meta.json --outfile=/dev/null

plus the files those import for their types only (found by `tsc -b`), which are checked but never bundled. ren desktop's
`@catalyst` alias is in vite.config.ts and tsconfig.ren.json.

Seven files come from ren desktop's fixes on top of b112adf (github.com/ImRonAI/TheBrowser pull 5, commit 96ff0d6):
components/agent-panel/ContextPicker.tsx, components/build/CodeEditor.tsx and components/build/studio/CodePane.tsx fix
the nine `tsc` errors ren desktop had at b112adf, so the pages type-check with none; components/home/FavoriteLinks.tsx
keeps Discover's Add form off the screen's edge; and pages/ExecutePage.tsx, pages/ProjectsIndexPage.tsx and
components/projects/CanonicalIssueWorkspace.tsx open ren's own pages through its tab store where Electron isn't.

The phone's search page (src/search/) mounts SearchResultsPage as it is and supplies what Electron supplies on the
desktop. The destinations page (src/destinations/) mounts HomePage on the tab its address names (Discover, Execute,
Documents, Calendar, Vibe, Build) and, as App.tsx's MainContent does, the Projects and Documents pages its tab store
opens, with SignInPage behind #signin: a story opens in the browser's tab, and the favorites are the app's (src/host.ts). The agent panel's chat (src/panel/) draws the conversation with those components
the way AgentPanel.tsx does.
