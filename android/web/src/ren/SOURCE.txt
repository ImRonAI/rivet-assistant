Ren's search results page, its home page (Discover and the environments) and its agent panel's components from ren
desktop, unmodified: github.com/ImRonAI/TheBrowser at b112adf.

  pages/SearchResultsPage.tsx and everything it imports     src/ (the same paths, without the src/ prefix)
  pages/HomePage.tsx, pages/OnboardingPage.tsx and theirs   src/
  the agent panel's components (below) and their imports    src/
  index.css, vite-env.d.ts, pages/types/electron.ts         src/
  data/*.json (Discover's bundled stories)                  ../data (src/data, with its own SHA256SUMS)
  tailwind.config.ts                                        the repository root

These files are never edited here (SHA256SUMS holds their hashes; `npm run check:ren` verifies them). The file list is
the import closure of src/pages/SearchResultsPage.tsx, src/pages/HomePage.tsx, src/pages/OnboardingPage.tsx and of the
components ren desktop's agent panel (src/components/agent-panel/AgentPanel.tsx) draws a conversation with:
ai-elements/conversation, message, response, chain-of-thought, tool and shimmer:

  esbuild src/pages/SearchResultsPage.tsx --bundle --packages=external --metafile=meta.json --outfile=/dev/null

plus the files those import for their types only (found by `tsc -b`), which are checked but never bundled. ren desktop's
`@catalyst` alias is in vite.config.ts and tsconfig.ren.json.

At b112adf ren desktop's own `tsc` reports nine errors, in components/agent-panel/ContextPicker.tsx and
components/build/studio/CodePane.tsx; they are listed in scripts/ren-tsc-known.txt, and scripts/tsc.mjs fails on any
other error and on any of them going away. The fix belongs in ren desktop.

The phone's search page (src/search/) mounts SearchResultsPage as it is and supplies what Electron supplies on the
desktop. Discover (src/discover/) mounts HomePage on its Discover tab: a story opens in the browser's tab, and the
favorites are the app's (src/host.ts). The agent panel's chat (src/panel/) draws the conversation with those components
the way AgentPanel.tsx does.
